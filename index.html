Who We Are

Sovereign Capital Supply is an Indigenous-owned business based in Victoria, supporting workforce coordination, contractor engagement, project support and Indigenous participation outcomes across Victoria and Australia. We work alongside contractors, project managers, councils, government agencies and Indigenous organisations to support successful project delivery and create meaningful opportunities across Victoria and Australia.

Our Leadership & Cultural Guidance

Sovereign Capital Supply is a family-owned Indigenous business built on strong family values, connection to Country and community. Sovereign Capital Supply is led by James R.C Mongta, Director, and Larry B Mongta, Partner & Indigenous Elder, as a family-owned Indigenous business built on strong values, cultural guidance and community connection. Larry provides cultural guidance, community connection and support to ensure Sovereign Capital Supply remains grounded in respect, integrity and positive outcomes for Indigenous communities. Together, we are committed to building opportunities, supporting project delivery and creating long-term benefits across Victoria and Australia.

Acknowledgement of Country

Sovereign Capital Supply acknowledges the Traditional Owners of the lands on which we live and work throughout Australia. We pay our respects to Elders past and present and recognise their continuing connection to land, waters, culture and community. As a 100% Indigenous family-owned business, we are committed to building respectful relationships and supporting opportunities that contribute to stronger communities and meaningful participation.

Lived Experience & Opportunity

Sovereign Capital Supply understands that real opportunity can change lives, families and communities. Our lived experience gives us a strong understanding of the barriers people can face when trying to rebuild, work and create a better future. We are committed to turning that understanding into practical pathways, workforce opportunities, strong partnerships and positive outcomes for Indigenous people, local communities and future generations.

Our Commitment

We are committed to supporting regional growth, local employment opportunities and meaningful Indigenous participation across Victoria and Australia. Our focus is on building strong partnerships, supporting project delivery and creating opportunities that contribute to sustainable economic growth, stronger communities and long-term regional development. We believe successful projects should create lasting benefits for local businesses, local people and future generations.

Our Vision

To become a trusted Indigenous-owned business supporting construction, civil and infrastructure projects while creating opportunities for Indigenous participation, workforce development and sustainable economic growth across Victoria and Australia.
