Who We Are

Sovereign Capital Supply is an Indigenous-owned Victorian business focused on supporting project delivery through workforce coordination, contractor engagement, project support and Indigenous participation outcomes. We work with contractors, project managers, councils, government agencies, suppliers and Indigenous organisations to help create successful project outcomes and long-term opportunities.
