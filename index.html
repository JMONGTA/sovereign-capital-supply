Who We Are

Sovereign Capital Supply is an Indigenous family-owned business focused on workforce coordination, contractor engagement, project support and Indigenous participation throughout Victoria. We work collaboratively with industry, government, contractors, suppliers and Indigenous organisations to support successful project delivery while creating meaningful community outcomes and long-term opportunities.

What We Do

Workforce Coordination

Supporting workforce mobilisation and labour solutions for projects across Victoria.

Contractor Engagement

Building partnerships between contractors, suppliers and project stakeholders.

Project Support

Providing support services that help projects achieve successful outcomes.

Indigenous Participation

Creating pathways for Indigenous business engagement, workforce participation and community outcomes.

Our Story

Sovereign Capital Supply was founded by James R.C. Mongta and Larry B. Mongta with a vision of creating opportunity for future generations. Built on Gunaikurnai heritage and operating on Yorta Yorta Country, Sovereign was established to support projects, workforce participation and Indigenous engagement across Victoria. Through resilience, lived experience and a commitment to positive change, Sovereign represents a journey toward building a stronger future for family, children, community and future generations. Our mission is to create sustainable opportunities, strengthen Indigenous participation and contribute to successful project delivery across Victoria.
