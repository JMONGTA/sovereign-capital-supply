Who We Are

Sovereign Capital Supply is a 100% Indigenous family-owned business focused on workforce coordination, contractor engagement, project support and Indigenous participation throughout Victoria. Founded by James R.C. Mongta and Larry B. Mongta, Sovereign was built on the belief that meaningful opportunity, responsibility and community create lasting change. We work alongside contractors, industry, government, suppliers and Indigenous organisations to help deliver successful projects while supporting workforce participation, community outcomes and long-term growth.

Our Story

Sovereign Capital Supply was established to create opportunities for family, community and future generations. Built on Gunaikurnai heritage and proudly operating on Yorta Yorta Country, the business reflects a commitment to resilience, accountability, leadership and positive change. Our goal is to build sustainable pathways into workforce participation, project delivery, Indigenous engagement and regional development across Victoria. We believe successful projects create lasting value when participation, community outcomes and project delivery work together.
