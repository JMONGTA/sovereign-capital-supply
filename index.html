Who We Are

Sovereign Capital Supply is an Indigenous-owned Victorian business focused on workforce coordination, contractor engagement, project support and Indigenous participation outcomes. We work alongside contractors, project managers, councils, government agencies and Indigenous organisations to support successful project delivery and create meaningful opportunities throughout Victoria.

Acknowledgement of Country

Sovereign Capital Supply acknowledges the Traditional Owners of the lands on which we live and work throughout Australia. We pay our respects to Elders past and present and recognise their continuing connection to land, waters, culture and community. As a 100% Indigenous family-owned business, we are committed to building respectful relationships and supporting opportunities that contribute to stronger communities and meaningful participation.

Our Commitment

We are committed to supporting regional growth, local employment opportunities and meaningful Indigenous participation throughout Victoria. Our focus is on building strong partnerships, supporting project delivery and creating opportunities that contribute to sustainable economic growth, stronger communities and long-term regional development. We believe successful projects should create lasting benefits for local businesses, local people and future generations.

Our Vision

To become a trusted Indigenous-owned business supporting construction, civil and infrastructure projects while creating opportunities for Indigenous participation, workforce development and sustainable economic growth throughout Victoria.

Industries We Support

We support construction, civil infrastructure, local government, regional development projects, Indigenous organisations, contractors, suppliers and workforce partners throughout Victoria.
