Who We Are

Sovereign Capital Supply is an Indigenous-owned Victorian business focused on workforce coordination, contractor engagement, project support and Indigenous participation outcomes. We work alongside industry, contractors, councils, government agencies and Indigenous organisations to support successful project delivery and create meaningful opportunities throughout Victoria.
