About Sovereign

Sovereign Capital Supply is a 100% Indigenous family-owned business founded to support project delivery while creating meaningful opportunities for Indigenous participation, workforce engagement and regional development across Victoria. We work alongside contractors, project managers, government agencies, councils, Indigenous organisations and industry partners to help connect projects with capability and support successful outcomes.

Our Services

Workforce Coordination

Supporting workforce mobilisation, coordination and project workforce requirements.

Contractor Engagement

Connecting projects with contractors, suppliers and industry capability.

Project Support

Providing practical support for construction, civil and infrastructure projects.

Procurement Support

Assisting projects with sourcing solutions and supplier engagement.

Logistics Coordination

Supporting project delivery through planning and logistical coordination.

Infrastructure Projects

Supporting roads, bridges, civil works, buildings and major developments.

Indigenous Participation

As an Indigenous-owned business, Sovereign Capital Supply is committed to supporting practical Indigenous participation outcomes through workforce pathways, supplier engagement, local capability development and community connection. Our objective is to contribute to stronger projects, stronger communities and long-term opportunity creation throughout Victoria.
